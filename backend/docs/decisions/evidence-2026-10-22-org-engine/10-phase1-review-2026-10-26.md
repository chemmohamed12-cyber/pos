1. `undoReleaseMigrations` under the pre-fix 6-tag registry **must reject** with `No migration … was found in the source code`;
2. the (mocked) full-restore fallback was consulted exactly once (`preflight` called once);
3. **the executed-migration count is identical to the count before the undo** — nothing was reverted or deleted by the failed undo;
4. then the fixed registry **does** undo (count strictly lower).

The original fixture satisfied that only by coincidence of timestamps; the cut-off makes it deterministic.
