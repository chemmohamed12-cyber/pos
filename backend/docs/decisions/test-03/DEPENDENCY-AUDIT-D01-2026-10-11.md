### 1b. Backend — dev-only (50 packages)
Almost entirely the Jest 29 tool chain (`@jest/*`, `babel-jest`, `jest-*`, `expect`), the Nest CLI/Angular schematics chain, `handlebars` (critical, via `ts-jest`), `brace-expansion`, `js-yaml`, `micromatch`/`picomatch`, `webpack`. They run on developer machines and CI only. Fix path: Jest 30 and Nest CLI 12 (both major) plus 13 non-breaking candidates.
### 1c. Frontend
`react-router` / `react-router-dom` (runtime, moderate): open redirect via backslash in `<Link>`/`useNavigate`; constructor injection in SSR hydration (DAII is a client-side SPA — SSR hydration is not used, INFERRED). `source-map-js` (dev, high): event-loop DoS in a build-time library. Fix: `react-router-dom@7.18.4` (major).
