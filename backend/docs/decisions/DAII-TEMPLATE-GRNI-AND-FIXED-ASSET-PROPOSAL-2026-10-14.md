## 1. `grni` — Goods Received Not Invoiced
| | |
|---|---|
| Who requires it | `goods-receipt.handler.ts` (Dr Inventory / Cr GRNI) rejects with "no GRNI control account in the chart — assign controlFunction "grni" to an account first"; `supplier-invoice.handler.ts` (Dr GRNI / Cr AP) rejects likewise; `InventoryPostingPolicyService.assertSupplierInvoicePostable` throws a conflict before a supplier invoice posts if no active postable `grni` account exists; `grni-reconciliation.service.ts` reports that no `grni` account exists. ## 2. `fixed_asset` — not one account
`fixed_asset` is a control function marking a *category's cost account and its Accumulated Depreciation contra account* (not singleton; one pair per category — `control-function.ts`). Three separate gaps follow from the code:
| Piece | Today | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| Cost / contra pair per category | the template has `1201 Property, Plant & Equipment` and `1202 Accumulated Depreciation` (contra) but **neither carries** `controlFunction = fixed_asset` | **Traced (read-only, 2026-10-14):** asset creation/update validates only the *shape* of the three accounts — cost: type `asset`, not contra; accumulated depreciation: type `asset`, contra; depreciation expense: type `expense` (`fixed-asset.service.ts`, `assertAccountShape`); nothing there or in the reconciliation requires `controlFunction = fixed_asset` (the reconciliation compares each stored cost/contra account pair; it never blends control-function accounts). The template already supplies all three shapes: `1201` (asset), `1202` (asset, contra), `5204 Depreciation Expense`. **So the cost/contra gap is not a failure mode**; what the marker would add is the standard control-account protection (`allowManualJournal = false` for any account with a control function), i.e. a guard against manual journals into the asset accounts |
| Disposal gain / loss accounts | configuration keys `accounting.fixed_asset_gain_on_disposal_account_code` / `…_loss_…`, deliberately defaulted to empty (FA-02: "no account code is chosen by the code"); the template has no gain-on-disposal revenue or loss-on-disposal expense row | a disposal is refused before it mutates anything until the owner configures usable accounts (verified, FA-02) |
| Acquisition clearing | key `accounting.fixed_asset_acquisition_clearing_account_code`, empty by default; no template row | acquisition posting is refused until configured |
**Options.** A: mark `1201`/`1202` with `fixed_asset` — adds manual-journal protection only; changes the meaning of two existing template rows. B: add new rows for gain on disposal (revenue), loss on disposal (expense) and acquisition clearing, and leave the three configuration keys unset (accounts exist, wiring stays the owner's). C: both. D: leave as is and document the onboarding steps (assets work today; disposal and acquisition postings are refused until the owner configures accounts — by design, verified in FA-02). **What the trace supports:** assets can already be created, depreciated and reconciled with template accounts; the real onboarding gap is the three configuration accounts, whose absence fails closed. Choosing between B and D is accounting policy (does the starter chart pre-create disposal and clearing accounts without defaulting the keys — against FA-02's "nothing defaulted"); A is optional hardening. No direction is selected here.
**Acceptance (when a direction is approved):** a fresh Company can create a category, acquire, depreciate and dispose using only template accounts plus the owner's configuration, with every event POSTED and the fixed-asset reconciliation matched; the pre-fix chart fails closed; isolation; regression.
